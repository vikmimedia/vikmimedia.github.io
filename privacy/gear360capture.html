Privacy Policy

Gear 360 Capture

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Gear 360 Capture ("the App"), developed by Vikmi Media. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may access, how that information is used, and how it is protected when you use the App.

Information We Collect

Photos and Media

  • Display panoramic images.
  • Save captured photos.
  • Manage images selected by you.

Location Information

Location permission may be required for Wi-Fi discovery and connection features on Android and map-related functionality.

Local Network Connection

The App communicates with Samsung Gear 360 (2016) SM-C200 through a local Wi-Fi connection using Google Street View mode.

Internet Connection

Internet access may be required for advertisements and online services.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Start.io. Advertisements may appear in different parts of the App, including the Welcome screen and other application screens.

Start.io may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • General usage information

More information:

Start.io Privacy Policy

How We Use Information

  • Connect with Samsung Gear 360 (SM-C200).
  • Capture 360° photos remotely.
  • Download images.
  • Display panoramic images.
  • Provide location-based functionality.
  • Display advertisements.
  • Improve application performance and stability.

Data Sharing

The App does not sell your personal information. Certain technical information may be shared with trusted third-party providers solely to operate the App.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13.
