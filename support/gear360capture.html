Gear 360 Capture Support

Everything you need to get started with Gear 360 Capture.

About

Gear 360 Capture allows you to connect your Android device to a Samsung Gear 360 (2016) camera using Google Street View mode.

Requirements

  • Samsung Gear 360 (2016)
  • Model SM-C200
  • Android device
  • Google Street View mode

Getting Started

  1. Turn on the Gear 360 camera.
  2. Enable Google Street View mode.
  3. Connect your Android phone to the Gear 360 Wi-Fi hotspot.
  4. Open Gear 360 Capture.
  5. Start taking 360° photos.

Main Features

  • Remote Capture
  • Download Images
  • Gallery Management
  • Easy Wi-Fi Connection

Troubleshooting

Camera not detected

Make sure your phone is connected to the Gear 360 Wi-Fi hotspot and the camera is running in Google Street View mode.

Cannot connect

Restart the camera and reconnect to its Wi-Fi hotspot before opening the application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which camera model is supported?

Samsung Gear 360 (2016) SM-C200.

Does it support normal Samsung connection mode?

No. Currently the application supports Google Street View mode only.

Can I download captured images?

Yes. Images can be downloaded directly from the camera.
